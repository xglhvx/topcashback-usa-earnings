## How Does Cash Back Actually Work?

This is the part most people get confused about, so let me walk through it as plainly as I can.

When you click "Activate Cash Back" on TopCashback and then shop at a partner store, your purchase gets tracked. The retailer pays TopCashback a commission for the referral. TopCashback then credits that commission to your account as cash back.

Your cash back shows up in your account in one of three statuses:

- **Pending** — the transaction is being tracked and confirmed by the retailer (this can take days to weeks depending on the merchant's return window).
- **Confirmed** — the retailer has confirmed the purchase, but the cash back hasn't become payable yet.
- **Payable** — the cash back is yours to withdraw whenever you want, however you want.

That last point is worth pausing on: TopCashback has **no minimum payout threshold** and **no payout schedule**. Whether you've earned $0.01 or $1,000, you can cash out whenever the money hits payable status. Rakuten, by contrast, pays quarterly and holds anything under $5.01 until the next cycle.
